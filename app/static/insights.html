22 July 2026

New Stamp Duty Rules for Digital Agreements

TL;DR E-stamped agreements now need an extra verification step from 1 August.

From 1 August, e-stamped agreements will require an additional verification step before they're accepted for registration. If you're finalising a sale deed, lease, or commercial contract in the coming weeks, build this extra step into your timeline so signing isn't delayed at the last mile.

What this means for you: if you have an agreement pending e-stamping, get it processed before 1 August where possible, or plan for the added verification time.

14 July 2026

What the Latest GST Circular Means for Freelancers

TL;DR Invoicing thresholds shift for service providers earning above ₹20L.

The latest circular changes invoicing thresholds for service providers crossing ₹20 lakh in annual earnings. Freelancers and independent consultants near this threshold should review their invoicing practices to stay compliant with the updated rules.

What this means for you: if your annual billing is approaching ₹20L, it's worth a quick compliance check before your next filing cycle.

02 July 2026

Tenant Rights After the Model Tenancy Update

TL;DR Security deposit caps and notice periods have changed.

Updated rules under the Model Tenancy framework adjust both the maximum security deposit landlords can collect and the notice period required before termination. Both landlords and tenants drafting new agreements should reflect these updated caps and timelines.

What this means for you: review any tenancy agreement you're about to sign or renew against the new deposit and notice-period limits.
